How to play

A new scene and a director's brief every day. Drag your lights around the floor plan, set height, output and colour, then roll camera. One take per difficulty per day, graded 0–100 against the director's own rig.

Easy
Spec sheet included, fixtures pre-chosen, forgiving.
Medium
A director's note in plain language. Hard or soft.
Expert
Scene and mood only. Every fixture, distance and gel. Strict.

Cheat sheet

Key
The main light. Its angle decides where shadows fall.
Fill
Weaker, opposite side, lifts the shadows.
Ratio
Lit side vs shadow side. 2:1 friendly, 4:1 cinematic, 8:1+ drama.
Back / rim
From behind, edges the subject off the background.
Kelvin
2000–3200K candle/tungsten, 5600K daylight, 7000K+ blue.
